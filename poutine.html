Poutine is a Quebec dish of french fries with cheese curds and hot gravy, first appearing in the centre-du-Quebec region around 1959, though it exact orgins are disputed. Once moked by some, it becomed celebrate d symbol of Quebecois culture and cousins and grew popular across Canada with many regional varitaions and annual festival.

Poutine history

Poutine began in rural Quebec in the late 1950s, though its exact origins and the source of its name are uncertain. Once seen as humble, informal food, it became a celebrated part of Quebec cuisine and Québécois identity, spread across Canada and beyond, and inspired many variations.
