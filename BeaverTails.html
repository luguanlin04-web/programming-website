What are Beaver Tails

Beavertails are a popular Canadian pastry. They are made from dough stretched into a flat, oval shape like a beaver's tail. The dough is deep-fried until golden and crispy. It is often topped with cinnamon sugar, chocolate, or fruit. Many people enjoy them at festivals and during winter activities.

Start of Beaver Tails

Beavertails became popular in 1978 when Grant and Pam Hooker sold them at a fair in Ontario. The recipe came from Grant's grandmother. In 1980, they opened their first store at Ottawa's ByWard Market. Later, Beavertails became popular at the Rideau Canal Skate. Today, they are a well-known Canadian treat.
