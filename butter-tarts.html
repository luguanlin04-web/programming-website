The sweet taste of Canada

Discover Butter tarts

A butter tart is a small Canadian pastry with a flaky crust and a sweet, buttery filling made from ingredients such as butter, sugar, and eggs. The middle is usually soft and gooey. Some bakers add raisins or currants, while others leave them out.

History of Butter tarts

The earliest known printed butter tart recipe appeared in a community cookbook from Barrie, Ontario, in 1900. It was called “Filling for Tarts.” The recipe may have been made in homes before then, but its exact origins are uncertain. Butter tarts became especially popular in Ontario, where bakers created many variations, including versions with maple syrup.
